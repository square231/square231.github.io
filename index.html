Cherie Browser

A personal web browser for iPhone, built on WKWebView. It has a URL field for entering addresses directly, search for finding pages on the web, and bookmarks for the sites I return to. It renders the page at the address I ask for — nothing more, nothing in between. It is a small, single-purpose app that I develop and use myself.

Chérie Mothership

My personal device-management app for iPhone. It pulls together tools I use to look after my own phone and my own home — notifications, media, files, and everyday utilities — in one place, under my control.
