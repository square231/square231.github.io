Privacy Policy

For Cherie Browser and Chérie Mothership. Last updated October 31, 2026.

These are personal apps that I build and use on my own devices. They are written to keep my data on my device, and to collect as little as possible — in practice, nothing.

What the apps collect

  • No analytics. Neither app measures or reports how it is used.
  • No telemetry. Neither app sends usage data, diagnostics, or crash information to me or to anyone else.
  • No tracking. Neither app tracks you across apps or websites, and neither contains advertising SDKs or third-party trackers.
  • No accounts. Neither app asks you to sign up, sign in, or provide personal information to use it.

Browsing data

Cherie Browser loads the pages you ask it to load, the same as any web browser. Your history, bookmarks, and any other browsing data stay on your device. Websites you visit can see the same information they would see from any browser — such as your IP address and browser details — because that is how the web works; the browser itself reports nothing about you to me.

Data sharing

Because the apps collect no data, there is no data to share, sell, or disclose to third parties.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
